PROJECT 01

THE PROBLEM

New students had to finish nine setup screens before seeing a single lesson. Many never made it, and the team did not know where or why they left.

THE GOAL

Get every new student to a first lesson in minutes, with only the setup that really matters, and keep the rest for later.

PROCESS

Image · 16:10

01

Research

Interviewed 18 students and mapped exactly where they dropped off.

Image · 16:10

02

Design

Cut nine setup screens down to four and put the first lesson up front.

Image · 16:10

03

Test

Tested two versions with 12,000 users and kept the stronger one.
